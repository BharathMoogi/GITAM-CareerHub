Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GITAM CareerHub access, features, and mentorship.

How do I get access to GITAM CareerHub?
All enrolled GITAM engineering students automatically receive access using their official GITAM university email credentials (`@gitam.in` or `@gitam.edu`). Simply click 'Login' and authenticate.
Which engineering branches are supported?
GITAM CareerHub supports all major engineering branches including Computer Science (CSE), Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML), Electronics & Communication (ECE), Electrical & Electronics (EEE), Mechanical Engineering, Robotics & Automation, and VLSI Design.
How does the AI Mentor work?
Our AI Mentor is trained on your exact academic profile, branch curriculum, completed projects, and target companies. It provides tailored project recommendations, resume bullet point enhancements, and mock interview coaching.
Does the platform track placement drive eligibility automatically?
Yes! The Internship & Placement Engine dynamically evaluates your branch, semester, CGPA, and readiness score against company criteria, letting you apply in one click with instant status tracking.
